I used AI to help me understand how ticket workflow operates in CampusFlow. I learned that tickets move through different statuses, including `open`, `in_progress`, and `resolved`.

### Interaction 2 — Ticket Assignment and Open-Ticket Queues

I used AI to improve my understanding of ticket assignment and open-ticket queues in CampusFlow. I learned that assignment helps identify the staff member responsible for handling a ticket, while the open-ticket queue helps staff find unresolved requests that need attention.

I also learned that workflow rules and queue filters must work together so staff can manage tickets correctly.

**Learning outcome:** I improved my understanding of how ticket assignment and queue management help organize support requests.
